MODULE 8 · EU AI ACT · RISK TIERS

A company plans to use AI to infer employees' emotions from webcam footage during meetings. What is the AI Act status of this use?

Answer: A. Emotion recognition in the workplace is a prohibited practice under the EU AI Act, except for medical or safety reasons. Employee consent does not lift the ban.
