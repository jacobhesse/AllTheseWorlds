Working Definition

What is Digital Transformation?

Digital transformation is the deliberate integration of digital technologies with new ways of working to reshape your operating model, value delivery, and culture. It goes beyond tools: it aligns strategy, processes, data, and people so that improvements are repeatable, secure, and adopted.

In plain terms: DT is how an organization modernizes how it creates value—standardizing and automating core processes, unlocking data for decisions, strengthening security and compliance, and enabling teams to work effectively across geographies.

Our perspective is grounded in current research and practice: adoption succeeds when leaders establish psychological readiness for change (commitment + efficacy), and when leadership styles fit team cultural expectations—especially for US–India delivery models.
