The control gap

Unmanaged AI use is not just an IT issue. It is an operating risk.

Most small organizations do not need an enterprise AI governance program. They need clear operating rules: what employees may use AI for, what information must never be entered, which tools require approval, and how AI-generated outputs must be reviewed before use.

!What goes wrong without rules
  • Employees paste client, customer, employee, legal, financial, health, or confidential business data into public AI tools.
  • AI-generated content is sent to clients, vendors, regulators, boards, donors, or the public without review.
  • Teams use unapproved AI browser extensions, meeting bots, document analyzers, image tools, or SaaS AI features without leadership visibility.
  • Managers are left guessing whether a use case is safe, restricted, prohibited, or requires approval.
✓What this kit helps put in place
  • A written AI acceptable use policy employees can understand and follow.
  • A clear approved, restricted, and prohibited AI use matrix for everyday decisions.
  • A lightweight tool approval process that does not require a full governance department.
  • A human review procedure so AI assists work without becoming the final decision-maker.

FAQ

Common buyer questions

Is this meant to ban AI at work?

No. The kit is designed to help organizations allow productive AI use while setting boundaries for sensitive data, tool approval, human review, and higher-risk use cases.

Is this a complete AI governance program?

No. It is a practical starter control system for small organizations. It does not replace legal, privacy, security, HR, or compliance review where those issues apply.

What kinds of AI tools does it cover?

It is written broadly for generative AI tools, including ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, embedded AI features in SaaS products, transcription tools, document analyzers, browser extensions, image generators, and similar tools.

Can the documents be edited?

Yes. The kit is intended to be delivered as editable Word documents and Excel-compatible trackers, with optional PDF versions for distribution.

Who should implement it?

Owners, operations leaders, office managers, HR, IT, compliance, managing partners, executive directors, or consultants can implement the kit. Very small organizations can use the minimum viable rollout path.
