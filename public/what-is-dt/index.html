What is DT?

Digital transformation is the coordinated process of reshaping how an organization works. It includes systems, workflows, leadership behaviors, and cross-cultural dynamics. Its result is that people can actually do better work with technology. It isn’t just implementing tools. It’s aligning leadership, culture, readiness, communication, and process so that individuals and teams (especially globally distributed ones, like U.S.–India teams) can adopt new digital systems with clarity, confidence, and shared purpose.

In my worldview, digital transformation succeeds only when the human layer evolves alongside the technical layer.
