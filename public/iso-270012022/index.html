The standard

What ISO 27001:2022 really asks for

ISO 27001:2022 is the global standard for building and maintaining an Information Security Management System (ISMS). It is not just a checklist of technical controls. It is a way of running your business so that risks to information are identified, prioritized, and treated in a structured, repeatable way.

At its core, the standard asks your organization to understand its context, identify the information that matters most, evaluate risks to that information, implement appropriate controls, and continuously improve. The 2022 revision modernized Annex A controls to reflect today’s realities: cloud services, remote work, identity and access management, supplier risks, and more.

A well-implemented ISMS does three things for you:

  • Connects information security to business strategy, contracts, and growth.
  • Gives leadership clear visibility into risk and clear justification for investing (or not) in specific controls.
  • Provides a defensible story for regulators, customers, and partners: “Here is how we protect your data.”

Certification is the formal proof, but the real value is a security practice that works every day, not just during the audit.

The journey

How ISO 27001:2022 certification typically unfolds

Every organization is different, but most ISO 27001 journeys follow a similar pattern: understand your current state, design and implement the ISMS, run it long enough to gather evidence, and then invite the certifying body in.

Stargazer Consulting can step in at any point, but we deliver the most value when we guide you through the full lifecycle — so that scope, risk, controls, and evidence all line up when the auditor arrives.

1
Assess
Gap analysis & current-state mapping
We review your existing policies, technical controls, vendor landscape, and documentation to identify gaps against ISO 27001:2022 and Annex A. You get a clear picture of where you are and what “good enough” looks like for certification.
2
Design
ISMS architecture & roadmap
Together we define ISMS scope, risk methodology, Statement of Applicability, and governance structure. We build a prioritized roadmap that sequences policies, processes, controls, and tools into realistic phases.
3
Implement
Controls, training & operations
We co-create or refine your security policies, implement or tune controls, roll out training, and launch the ISMS calendar: risk reviews, internal audits, corrective actions, supplier reviews, and management reviews.
4
Certify
Audit preparation & support
We help you select a certification body, prepare evidence and narratives, brief leaders on what to expect, and support you through Stage 1 and Stage 2 audits. After certification, we refine your ISMS based on audit findings and lessons learned.

Already started on ISO 27001? We can plug into a specific phase (for example, risk assessment, Statement of Applicability, or internal audit) and help you close the loop.
