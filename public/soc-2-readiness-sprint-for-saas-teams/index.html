Stargazer Consulting | SOC 2 Readiness

SOC 2 readiness for SaaS teams without derailing engineering

For growth-stage SaaS companies, the hard part of SOC 2 is usually not the audit itself. It is the hidden engineering load, unclear ownership, competing priorities, and the slow drift into reactive work.

This readiness sprint is designed to give your team a practical path forward: a clear assessment of where you stand, what matters most, what can wait, and how to move toward SOC 2 without turning compliance into a constant interruption.

Book a Call See What’s Included

Who this is for

Growth-stage B2B SaaS companies, typically in the 30–100 employee range, preparing for a first SOC 2 initiative to support enterprise sales and customer trust.

The real problem

SOC 2 efforts often become messy because engineering, security, operations, and leadership are not aligned on scope, ownership, sequencing, or effort.

The outcome

A 30-day readiness sprint that gives you an actionable roadmap, prioritized risks, and a clearer path to execution with less disruption.

The pattern most CTOs run into

What usually happens

  • SOC 2 becomes “important,” but no one defines ownership clearly.
  • Engineering gets pulled into unplanned work with little context.
  • Policies, controls, vendors, and evidence requests start appearing all at once.
  • Momentum stalls because nobody is sure what should happen first.

What this causes

  • Lost engineering time and unnecessary interruption
  • Rework caused by poor sequencing
  • Confusion across teams about accountability
  • Longer, more frustrating paths to audit readiness

A focused 30-day readiness sprint

This is not generic compliance consulting. It is a focused engagement meant to help your team understand where you are, where the risk actually is, and how to move forward in a way that is realistic for your stage.

Minimize engineering disruption by defining what truly requires technical effort and what does not.
Clarify ownership across engineering, leadership, operations, and security-related responsibilities.
Reduce wasted effort by sequencing the work in a practical, execution-focused order.
Create a roadmap your team can actually use rather than a vague list of control requirements.

What’s included

Core deliverables

  • SOC 2 readiness gap assessment
  • Prioritized risk scorecard
  • 30–90 day execution roadmap
  • Core policy starter set

Execution layer

  • Ownership and accountability mapping
  • Identification of likely bottlenecks and friction points
  • Analysis of where teams typically lose momentum
  • Recommendations to reduce coordination breakdowns

Why this approach is different

Most SOC 2 support focuses on controls, documentation, and audit mechanics. Those things matter, but they are not usually the reason a growth-stage SaaS team gets bogged down.

The bigger issue is execution. Work gets diffused across teams. Engineering absorbs effort it did not plan for. Leadership underestimates the coordination burden. The process becomes heavier than it needed to be.

This readiness sprint addresses the technical and operational side of SOC 2, but it also focuses on how the work actually moves through your organization.
