Solar/Sizer

Pick a use case, describe what you run, and get panel count, battery bank, inverter, the parts in between, and a 2026 cost range.

Estimates for planning, not a design. Electricity rates are EIA state averages (March 2026, preliminary); your utility’s rate may differ. Sun hours come from NREL PVWatts when available, otherwise from state-level approximations; for a specific roof run NREL PVWatts. Electrical work tied to a home or the grid needs a permit and, in most places, a licensed installer. Wire and fuse sizes assume short copper runs and must be checked against NEC ampacity tables for your run length and temperature.
