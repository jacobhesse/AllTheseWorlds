Halden Analytics, a fictional 28-person SaaS company.

The workbook ships blank and fully completed for Halden, and the guide walks through its decisions. Here is how one system moved through the loop.

  1. RequestThe People Lead wants to pilot an AI résumé screener. She raises a request, as the acceptable use standard requires.
  2. Triage: T1Its rankings influence hiring decisions. Flags: employment, EU AI Act high-risk, Colorado automated decision tool.
  3. Vendor review: conditions requiredScores 87%, but two critical answers are weak. The vendor cannot confirm it won’t train on applicant data, and it hasn’t shared bias-test results.
  4. AssessmentSix risks and five impacts on applicants. The biggest risk drops from 15 to 8 with recruiter review and a quarterly bias check.
  5. DecisionThe CEO approves a pilot to year end with recorded conditions. One row in the inventory now shows the whole trail.

Questions

Before you buy

Will this get us ISO/IEC 42001 certified?

No kit can do that. Certification comes from an accredited body that audits a management system once it has been running. Throughline gives you the core of that system: policy, roles, inventory, risk and impact assessment, supplier control and records. Certification Prep adds the remaining documents most audits look for.

Do we need ISO 27001 first?

No. Throughline is designed to plug into ISO 27001 and uses its risk scale, incident process and management review. If you run SOC 2 or a less formal security program, use the same hooks in your own processes.

What do we actually get?

Excel and Word files you edit and keep, plus a web questionnaire for vendors. There is no software to install and no account to create. Your data stays in your files.

Does it cover the EU AI Act and US state laws?

Every AI system gets regulatory flags, and assessments include an EU AI Act screen. The acceptable use standard includes the chat-tool clause Colorado’s 2027 law looks for. Treat them as prompts for a conversation with counsel.

How long does it take?

The guide is built around 30 days for a company with 5 to 15 AI tools. If a customer questionnaire is due sooner, start with the inventory and the answer bank’s fallback wording.

Is this legal advice?

No. It is practitioner-built governance material. We recommend a short legal review of the policy clauses flagged in the guidance notes.

Can consultants use it with clients?

A consultant licence is planned. Get in touch through alltheseworlds.us if you want early access.
