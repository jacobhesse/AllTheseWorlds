Why this kit matters before you hire a fractional CISO
Most organizations know they “should do more” for cybersecurity but don’t have a clear picture of what “good” looks like for their size and risk profile. Before you commit to any provider, this kit helps you map where you are today, what you actually need, and how a fractional CISO fits into your existing leadership and technical stack.

Use it internally with your leadership team

  • Run a 30-minute working session with your C-suite or partners using the scorecard.
  • Align on your top three security outcomes for the next 90 days.
  • Decide whether you need advisory, ongoing leadership, or both.

Clarify if fractional CISO is the right move

  • Avoid over-buying tools when your real gap is leadership and accountability.
  • See what a fractional CISO would own versus your internal IT/engineering team.
  • Walk into vendor conversations with a clear, prioritized set of needs.
